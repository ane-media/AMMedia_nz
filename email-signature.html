Add it to Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "AM Media NZ".
  3. Paste into the box (Cmd+V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes.
