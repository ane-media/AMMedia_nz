Add it to Spark

Spark takes the signature as HTML code. Copy the code with the button, then paste it in.

Mac:

  1. Open Spark Settings, then Composer, then Signatures.
  2. Click the + (create) button at the top right.
  3. Click HTML at the bottom right and paste the code.
  4. Save. Under Default Signature, pick it for your ammedianz.com account.

iPhone:

  1. Tap the menu at the top left, then Settings, then Signatures. Turn on Use Email Signatures.
  2. Tap Add Signature, then HTML at the top right, and paste the code.
  3. Save. Then Settings → Mail Accounts → your account → Default Signature → tick it.

Using Gmail instead?

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "AM Media NZ".
  3. Paste into the box (Cmd+V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes.
