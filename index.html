About

Kia ora, I'm Ane.

Photographer, filmmaker and professional moment-catcher, based in Papamoa and happy to travel anywhere in Aotearoa.

I love reading people: the laugh building, the hug about to happen, the second before the finish line. Spotting those moments is the fun part. Nailing them in focus is the job, and I take that bit very seriously.

The big dream? Shooting action sport on the world's biggest stages. Every job, from a Saturday-morning game day to a skate session at the Mount, sharpens the eye for it.
